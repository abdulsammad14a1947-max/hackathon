3  Business rules
 Module
Rules that decide whether it works
M1 Auth
JWT login, bcrypt passwords. Every API checks role and data scope: an Employee only reads rows with their own
employee_id.
M2 Setup
Office start 09:00, grace 15 min, half day under 5 h, weekend Sat–Sun, office location + radius (e.g. 150 m). Stored as settings,
applied to new records only.
M3 Employees
HR registers each employee with job details and salary; this creates the employee’s login (temporary password, change on first
sign-in). Server-side search, filters, paging. Optional CSV import has a dry run returning row + field + reason; checks required
fields, unique code/email, valid email, known dept, ISO date, salary > 0.
M4 Attendance
Server stamps the time. One record per employee per day (second check-in = 409). Late after 09:15; half_day under 5 h;
absent if no record and no approved leave; missing_checkout if no check-out.
M5 Leave
Working days only (skip weekends, holidays). Quotas annual 14, sick 8, casual 10; unpaid unlimited. Overlap or over-balance
= 422.
M6 Payroll
Gross = 1.5 × basic (HRA 40%, medical 10%). Per day = gross ÷ working days; deduct absent + unpaid + 0.5 × half days.
Prorate joiners/leavers. Tax slab on gross × 12 (illustrative): 0 to 600k; 5% to 1.2M; 30k + 15% to 2.4M; 210k + 25% to 3.6M;
510k + 35% above. Admin finalizes; finalized runs are locked.
M7 Dashboards
All numbers computed on the server (database aggregation) and filterable by date range and department (see Section 7).
M9 Comms & Audit
Announcements on all dashboards, in-app notifications, audit log with actor, action, entity, before/after JSON.

Guardrails

9  Demo and judging
5-minute demo script
 Start from a fresh install: Admin sets up the company, office hours and location, a department and leave types, then creates an HR
login.
 HR registers two or three employees; one of them signs in, changes the password and checks in.
 Employee types a leave request in plain words and confirms the pre-filled form; HR approves it.
 HR opens the dashboard, clicks Summarize and asks the assistant who is on leave; runs payroll for the month; Admin finalizes it.
 Employee downloads the payslip and asks for a colleague’s salary (refused); Admin shows the audit log.
Scoring (100 points + 10 bonus)
